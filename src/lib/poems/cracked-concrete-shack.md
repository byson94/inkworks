---
title: Cracked Concrete Shack
collection: Green Poetry
date: 2026-11-28
---

Awaken I am in a concrete shack,
A crack that formed in the merry track.
I peek outside at a world in blue,
I am one of my kind in this tiny view.

I dreamt and I dreamt of soaring the skies,
A big green head that is ready to rise.
I shall crown the sky with gems in green,
In a way of course, that is to be seen.

My splendid growth that never slowed,
Attracted the foe that wanted me plowed.
I fell broken before his smiting sin,
Afar I lay in defeat, like my late kin.

The sky turned black like a slight frown,
For it lost its long awaited green crown.
Fell from the sky some pearls so pure,
It touched my soul, like the perfect cure.

Two weeks passed and ground erupts,
The soil scatter, with my cunning cuts.
Declare I am, to conquer the silver skies,
A bigger green head, that is ready to rise.
